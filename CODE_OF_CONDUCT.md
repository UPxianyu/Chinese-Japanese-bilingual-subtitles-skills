# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, hate speech, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reported incidents will be reviewed and investigated confidentially.
